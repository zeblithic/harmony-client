The Cascadia Commons Charter

✓ Ratified v4amended 12 days ago · P-12142 members bound
Preamble

Cascadia Commons stewards a shared mutual-aid treasury, a community space, and the agreements that bind us. This charter is our constitution. Every clause is visible to every member, and every clause can be changed by the members it governs.

Article I · Membership & roles

Roles are earned, granted, and revoked as a numeric power level. Three named bands:

MEMBERpower 0
Everyone who joins. Posts, votes, proposes, delegates — and may fork at any time.
MODERATORpower ≥ 50
Tends the space: channels, invites, join requests, and removing members below their level.
ADMINpower ≥ 100
Sets roles, changes the decision rules, and co-signs admin actions under quorum.

Article II · How we decide

Proposals move through three tiers. Higher stakes, higher bar.

Tier 1 · Pulse
Non-binding sentiment checks. No quorum. Anyone can open.
Tier 2 · Motion
Binding. Simple majority + 50% quorum. Conviction grows with time held. Delegable.
Tier 3 · Charter
Amends this charter. ⅔ supermajority + 60% quorum, 7-day floor.
Proposal quorum71 / 142
50% of members must vote for a Tier-2 motion to count.
Admin quorum3 of 4
3 of 4 admins must co-sign admin actions. No single admin can act alone.
✎Article III · Amendment

No clause here is permanent. Any member may open a Tier-3 proposal to amend the charter; if it passes, the new version is ratified and signed by the membership. Every prior version stays on the record — the charter is versioned, never overwritten. v1 → v4, 3 amendments, all public.

The charter is the product's thesis made visible. Power numbers become named roles; the buried admin quorum becomes a headline "3 of 4 · no single admin can act alone." Members can read their own constitution — and the "Propose amendment" button means they can change it.
Tiers map onto what's already in the code (Tier 2 motions, Tier 3 / conviction). I invented the ⅔/60% numbers as sensible defaults — every one should be a charter field, editable per community.
