Harmony · Design exploration · v1

Three directions for Harmony.

You're not building a Discord clone — you're building the place where a community forms, grows, and governs its commons. The current UI wears Discord's blurple-and-graphite off the rack: capable, but it says nothing about self-sovereignty, and nothing about the civic engine underneath. These three directions all sit inside your brief — calm & humane + civic & institutional, governance that reads as a serious voting tool, dense & pro — but each makes a different bet on tone, palette, type, and how navigation surfaces the governance layer.

A · Commons
Warm paper & ink, pastoral sage. A well-run co-op or public library. Governance lives in a right-hand Assembly rail.
B · Assembly
Cooler, architectural, ballot-serious. Governance gets equal billing with chat via a community status bar.
C · Chord
Soft, modern-warm, friendly. An icon mode-rail; proposals surface inline in the channel where the conversation is.
Brand direction · starting point
three centers, one consonance
Harmony alpha

Harmony is many independent voices resolving into one chord — polycentric, not centralized. The mark is three overlapping circles (three centers of power) meeting at a single shared point: the commons. No voice is dissolved into the others; together they're consonant.

Positioning line: "Form. Grow. Govern your commons." — a sidecar where a community can coordinate and act when the systems above it are too slow to.
