Exit, with your history intact
Harmony's most distinctive governance primitive. When a faction can't win a vote, they aren't silenced or captured — they fork: a new community branches off carrying a frozen snapshot of the shared past, while the original continues untouched. The split is written permanently into every channel's timeline, and a lineage tree records the whole genealogy. Exit becomes a credible check that keeps power honest.

Exit as a feature, not a failure. The tree reframes a "split" as a healthy branch in a family tree. Sage edges = amicable, clay edges = dispute — both legitimate. Nodes you belong to are tappable and badged ✓ Member.
Lineage is a check on power. A community that overreaches can be forked away from — the credible threat of exit (Hirschman) keeps facilitators honest. The genealogy can never be rewritten.

A fork writes an immutable divider into the new community's timeline: "Forked from Cascadia Commons · today." You become its first facilitator. This can't be undone — but the two communities can always cross-link or merge later.
Consent, not capture. Members are invited, never dragged. History is always carried (you can't erase the shared past), and the "why" is mandatory — a fork is an act of record, not a tantrum.
