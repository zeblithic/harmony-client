Reallocate $400 from reserves to the winter heating fund

Proposed by Heating WG·Treasury motion·Simple majority + quorum

Our reserve sits at $2,140 against a winter we expect to be hard. This motion moves $400 into the heating fund so the mutual-aid working group can cover emergency heating costs for members through March without a second vote each time.

Reserve floor stays above the $1,500 we agreed to hold in P-09. The Heating WG reports back monthly in #mutual-aid; unspent funds return to reserves in April.

Why now
Two members have already raised heating emergencies this month and the fund is empty. Acting before the cold snap beats reacting after.
