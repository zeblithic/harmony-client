Harmony · Design System · "Commons"

Foundations & components

A warm, civic system for communities to form, grow, and govern their commons. Tokens ship light & dark; every value is shown as a named token you can drop straight into app.css.

01 · Color

Two themes, one warm temperature

Neutrals are warm (paper, not paper-white; ink, not black). Saturation stays low everywhere except the three semantic accents. The dark theme is a warm near-black — brown-tinted, never Discord graphite.

02 · Typography

A serif for gravitas, a civic sans for work

Newsreader (display serif) gives community names and headers a deliberate, almost editorial weight. Public Sans — the U.S. government's own UI typeface — handles every interface need and signals "civic" without a word. IBM Plex Mono carries data: tallies, IDs, timestamps, addresses.
