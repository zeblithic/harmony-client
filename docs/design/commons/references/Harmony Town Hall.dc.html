This is the screen Discord can't have. Voice isn't just hanging out — it's a deliberative body. The speaker queue, the active-speaker spotlight, and "Call this to a motion" turn a conversation into a binding decision without anyone leaving the room.
Quorum-aware. If enough members are present, the motion can be voted live; if not, it gracefully becomes a 48-hour async proposal — so a sparsely-attended call can't railroad the whole commons.
