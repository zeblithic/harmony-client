Transitive & cycle-safe. Your vote follows the chain and you can trace the whole path; loops are blocked automatically.
Bottom tabs: Chat · Assembly · Activity · You. Governance gets a permanent home so a member can act on a vote in two taps from anywhere — the mobile answer to "voting should be as easy as messaging."
Drawer, not rail. The desktop's left column becomes a swipe-in Spaces drawer; communities still expand to channels in place, so the mental model is identical across devices.
Thumb-zone ballot. The vote bar pins to the bottom of the proposal so the primary action is always reachable; "Recall & vote myself" sits right above it.
That's the set: design system (light + dark tokens) → three directions → the chosen Commons direction taken to hi-fi on desktop and mobile, end-to-end through the governance loop. Open threads if you want them: onboarding & identity/backup, community settings & the fork/lineage tree, the Vines/Files/Mint side-modes, dark-theme renders of these screens, or an interactive clickable prototype of the vote flow.
