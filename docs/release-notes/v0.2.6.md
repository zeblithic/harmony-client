# Harmony v0.2.6

**A resilience-and-correctness release.** Where v0.2.5 was a big visible redesign, v0.2.6 mostly hardens the layers underneath: peer-to-peer connections that recover on their own, communities that heal after a network split, and moderation controls that match each community's actual rules. Roughly 39 changes since v0.2.5.

> ⚠️ **Alpha software — and self-sovereign by design.** Harmony is peer-to-peer: **your identity lives only on your own devices.** If you haven't already, open **Settings → Account** and export your recovery kit — there is no server-side account recovery. Expect rough edges, and please report what you find.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- **Communities that heal themselves.** When a network split leaves part of a community stranded, Harmony now detects the split by coverage, bridges every open slot to reconnect the halves, and paroles dormant peers back in. Membership converges instead of quietly fragmenting.
- **Joining is faster and more honest.** Invite redemptions that used to stall now re-attempt the moment a peer becomes reachable — cutting convergence from minutes to a single round-trip — and a redeem can be finalized by *any* joined member, not just the original inviter. Fewer false "inviter unreachable" dead-ends.
- **Connections that scale without flooding.** A new bounded-degree topology keeps each peer's connection set capped as communities grow, so large communities stop paying an ever-rising connectivity cost. (Validated for churn and reconvergence; the more aggressive router-mode remains behind a flag.)
- **Moderation controls that match your rules.** Per-community power thresholds now drive the actual controls you see: if your community lowered the kick threshold, authorized moderators finally see the kick control; the manage-members overlay is properly gated to moderators and up. What the UI offers now mirrors what the network will actually accept.
- **Keys that rotate cleanly.** Presence, the address book, channel messages, open-join admission, and rendezvous beacons all now follow the community's live rotating epoch key — so key changes take effect everywhere without stale-key gaps.

## 🔧 Under the hood

- **Leaner governance sync.** The voting log now reconciles by range instead of periodically re-dumping its full state, spawns eagerly at join and boot, and streams large state-roots off the single-writer path — meaningfully less bandwidth and no receiver stalls on big votes.
- **Bounded retention everywhere.** Serve-allowlist leases, buddy-pact records, relay holds, and the DM inbox all now expire on a disciplined TTL, so local state stops growing without bound (and can't be resurrected by a stale merge).
- **Deterministic network simulation harness.** New internal test infrastructure can partition, heal, and converge a simulated N-node community deterministically — the foundation for testing the resilience work above without a live fleet.
- **pkarr / DNS discipline.** Separated publish/fetch timeouts and a record budget derived from the real 1000-byte DNS-packet limit.

---

## 🧪 What to test

- **Community healing:** with two peers on genuinely separate networks, sever and restore connectivity — confirm membership reconverges rather than staying split.
- **Invite flow:** send an invite, redeem it while the inviter is briefly offline, confirm it completes when reachability returns (and check the redeem can be completed by a second joined member).
- **Moderation:** in a community with a customized kick or set-power threshold, confirm the kebab controls on member rows match who's actually authorized (and that a banned member's Unban shows for authorized moderators).
- **Everyday paths:** DMs, presence, voice — confirm nothing regressed under the key-rotation changes.
